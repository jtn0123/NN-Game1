# Crystal Caves gameplay polish — 2026-10-07

This is the actionable list for the game portion of NN-Game1. The comparison target is the original 1991 game. Changes are on `polish/crystal-caves-gameplay-20261007`; the accepted artwork and training maps are preserved.

**Baseline:** game experience B−, original feel C+. **After twenty-six passes:** game experience B, original feel B−. Passes 22–26 improve C09’s cover, dinosaur patrol and ammo choice. Five complete armed routes now win with three hearts and two ammo; an ammo-saving route wins with two hearts and seven ammo. C01’s seven three-heart wins remain green. Cave design holds at B−: fourteen complete cave routes, a dark-cave puzzle and original timing calibration remain. A cave marked “changed” has a specific improvement, not a claim that every player’s complete route has been perfected.

Coordinates below are **zero-based `(column,row)`**, measured from the top-left of the 40×24 cave. Cave numbers are the one-based numbers shown in the game. `P` means the lowercase `p` powered-shot pickup; FREEZE means `z`. Changes are explicit overlays in `src/unity_bridge/classic_polish.py`.
